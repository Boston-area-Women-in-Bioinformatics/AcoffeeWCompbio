## Description

Bioinformatics is a field built on collaboration and some of the most meaningful work happens beyond the lab and workplace.
In our latest podcast episode, we chat with our two wonderful guests Ryan Yordanoff from Cambridge biotech club and Aaron Blotnick from Bits in Bio and SynbioBeta. Together, we explore the people and communities working to make science more accessible, inclusive, and impactful. From creating opportunities for students and early-career researchers to building supportive communities, these organizations play an important role in shaping the future of bioinformatics. Tune in as we talk about the impact of volunteering, community building, and giving back to the bioinformatics community.
Because science isn't just about the work we do, it's also about the communities we build along the way!
