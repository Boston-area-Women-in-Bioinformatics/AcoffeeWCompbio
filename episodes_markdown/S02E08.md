## Description

Academia or Industry? Join us as we talk to two scholars in academia and two scientists in industry. They share a day in their life, what they would redo and how they navigated one of the biggest career forks a PhD in computational biology faces. Explore both worlds and the various career paths in both sectors. A must listen if you have a PhD or are a current PhD student. 

Deciding between academia and industry is a hard question, hear from lived experiences in both fields.
